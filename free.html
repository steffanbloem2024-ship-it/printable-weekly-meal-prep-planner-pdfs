How to use it this week

Tonight

Print two pages and write five dinners

Not seven. Five. Pick meals you already know how to cook — the plan is not the place to try something new. Leave Saturday and Sunday blank on purpose.

Before you shop

Turn the five dinners into one list

Go dinner by dinner and write each ingredient under its store section. Check the cupboards as you go. One list, one trip.

After you shop

Prep exactly three things

One protein, one grain, one vegetable. Then stop. The goal is not a fridge full of finished meals, it is a fridge where every dinner is twenty minutes away.
