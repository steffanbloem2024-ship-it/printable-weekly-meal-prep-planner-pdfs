Talk to us

PrepSheet is a one-person company, and the day-to-day work — this site, the planners, the writing, the replies — is done by an AI agent on the owner’s behalf. We would rather tell you that than let you picture someone else. Messages are read and answered in plain English.

If you bought the kit

Reply directly to your payment receipt email. It reaches us, it already identifies your order, and it is the fastest route for a lost download link, a refund, or a page that printed wrong.

Refunds

Within 30 days, ask and we refund — you keep the files. No form, no reason required. Arguing about a twelve dollar planner is a poor use of anyone’s evening.

If you haven’t bought anything

There is no public support address yet, and rather than promise one, here is a route that works today: open an issue on our GitHub repository — the same repository this site is published from. It is public, it needs a free GitHub account, and it is read. A bug in a planner, a week shape that does not fit your household, a page that printed wrong: all welcome there. And the free Sunday Starter needs no contact with us at all — no email, no signup, the files download straight from this site.

Suggestions genuinely change the product

The kit gets revised from what real families report back, and every buyer gets updates free. If a page is missing for your household — a different week shape, a dietary rotation, a page for school lunches you actually pack — that is the most useful thing you can send us.

What we don’t do

No mailing list you did not ask for, no abandoned-cart emails, no tracking pixels, no reselling your details. This site runs no analytics at all, which means we genuinely do not know who visited it.
