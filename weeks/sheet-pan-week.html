On the night

Monday — Sheet-Pan Chicken Thighs, Potatoes & Green Beans
Toss thighs and halved potatoes in oil, salt, pepper and paprika. 425°F / 220°C for 30 min; add green beans for the last 10.
Tuesday — Sheet-Pan Sausage, Peppers & Potatoes
Everything on one oiled sheet pan, 425°F / 220°C, 30 min, stir once.
Wednesday — Sheet-Pan Fish Tacos
Bake the cod with cumin and lime, 400°F / 205°C, 12 min. Flake into warm tortillas with slaw and sour cream.
Thursday — Roast Chicken Thighs with Cabbage & Carrots
Thighs skin-side up on a bed of wedged cabbage and carrots, oil, salt, 425°F / 220°C, 35 min. The chicken fat does the seasoning.
Friday — Lentil Shepherd’s Pie
Simmer lentils with carrot, onion and tomato paste, top with mashed potato, bake 25 min until the top browns.

The prep block — do these once, before the week starts

  • Halve the potatoes and trim the green beans.
  • Cut the potatoes and peppers.
  • Shred the cabbage slaw; dress it the morning of.
  • Wedge the cabbage, peel the carrots.
  • Boil and mash the potatoes — this is the slow part, so get it done ahead.Friday is five days after a weekend prep day, and cooked food keeps three to four. Either do this step on Wednesday instead, or freeze it on prep day and thaw it in the fridge the night before.

Why this week

There are two different kinds of tired. One wants dinner in fifteen minutes. The other does not mind waiting forty, as long as nobody needs anything from you while it happens — because that is the forty minutes you spend on homework, the bath, the email you owe, or sitting down.

This week is for the second kind. Four of the five nights are: cut a few things, put them on a tray, set a timer, walk away — and the washing up is a tray and a chopping board. The shepherd’s pie is the one that needs assembling, which is exactly why its mash moves into the prep block.

How to shuffle it

  • Put Lentil Shepherd’s Pie on the day you get home earliest — it is the longest night at 45 minutes, and it is the one dish here that reheats well the next day.
  • The two potato-and-tray nights (chicken thighs, and sausage with peppers) use the same technique and the same tray. Run them on your two hardest evenings.
  • Sheet-Pan Fish Tacos is the quick one at 20 minutes. Keep it in reserve for the evening that goes wrong.
  • Leave Saturday and Sunday open on purpose. Five planned dinners and two open nights is the whole method — a seven-box plan fails by Thursday and takes the rest of the week down with it.

Being straight with you: Hands-off is not the same as fast: these run 20–45 minutes in the oven, so start them when you walk in rather than when everyone is hungry. Oven temperatures are for a conventional oven — drop roughly 20°C / 25°F for a fan oven.
