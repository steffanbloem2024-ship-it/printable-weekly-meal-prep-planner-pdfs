On the night

Monday — Taco Night
Brown the beef with taco seasoning. Put the toppings on the table and let everyone build their own.
Tuesday — One-Pot Creamy Chicken & Orzo
Sear the chicken pieces, add orzo, stock and spinach, simmer 12 min, finish with cream and parmesan.
Wednesday — Big Skillet Fried Rice
Day-old rice, frozen mixed veg, scrambled egg, soy sauce, and anything left in the fridge.
Thursday — Pasta e Fagioli
Soften onion, carrot and celery, add crushed tomatoes, white beans, stock and small pasta. Simmer until the pasta is done. Parmesan on top.
Friday — One-Pot Chili Mac
Brown beef and onion in a big pot, add chili powder, crushed tomatoes, beans, 2 cups stock and the dry macaroni. Simmer covered 12 min. Stir in cheese.

The prep block — do these once, before the week starts

  • Shred the cheese and lettuce; dice the tomatoes into one container.
  • Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces.
  • Cook the rice ahead so it dries out — fresh rice goes gluey.
  • Dice the soup vegetables all at once.
  • Dice the onions.

Prep components, not finished meals: one protein, one grain, one vegetable survives a week that changes.

Why this week

Some weeks the cooking is fine and the washing up is the thing that breaks you — the colander, the second saucepan, the frying pan with the burnt bit, all waiting at nine o’clock. This week is built so the sink holds one pot, a board and a knife.

The pasta here cooks in the sauce rather than in its own water: the macaroni simmers in the chili, the orzo in the stock with the chicken, the small pasta in the bean soup. That is where the saved pot comes from, and it is also why these three thicken as they stand — add a splash of water when you reheat leftovers.

How to shuffle it

  • Big Skillet Fried Rice needs rice that was cooked a day or two earlier, so it cannot be Monday. Cook the rice in the prep block and put this on Wednesday or Thursday.
  • The two beef nights (Taco Night and Chili Mac) are best kept apart. The ground beef is merged into one 3 lb line on the list; freeze the 1 lb that is for the chili if it will not be cooked within two days.
  • Pasta e Fagioli is the meat-free night and the one that reheats best. Make it on your earliest evening and lunch is sorted the next day.
  • Leave Saturday and Sunday open on purpose. Five planned dinners and two open nights is the whole method — a seven-box plan fails by Thursday and takes the rest of the week down with it.

Being straight with you: “One pot” means one pot on the night: the fried rice needs its rice cooked ahead, which is one more pot during the prep block, and taco night puts a few bowls on the table. It is omnivore, contains dairy, gluten and egg, and is not allergen-aware — read every line before you shop if you are cooking around an allergy.
