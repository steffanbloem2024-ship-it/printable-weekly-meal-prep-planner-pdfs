On the night

Monday — Beef & Rice Burrito Bowls
Brown the beef. Build bowls with rice, black beans, corn, cheese and whatever toppings are around.
Tuesday — Chicken Quesadillas + Tomato Soup
Fill the tortillas with shredded chicken and cheese, crisp in a dry pan. Heat the soup.
Wednesday — Honey-Soy Salmon, Rice & Cucumber Salad
Brush the salmon with the sauce, bake 400°F / 205°C for 12 min. Reheat rice. Dress cucumbers with rice vinegar and a pinch of sugar.
Thursday — Breakfast for Dinner
Scrambled eggs with peppers and cheese, toast, sausages if you have them, a bowl of fruit on the table.
Friday — Egg Fried Rice
Leftover rice, frozen peas and carrots, three scrambled eggs, soy sauce, a little sesame oil.

The prep block — do these once, before the week starts

  • Cook the rice.Cooked food keeps three to four days: keep what Monday and Wednesday need in the fridge, and for Friday either cook a fresh batch midweek or freeze that portion on prep day and thaw it in the fridge the night before.
  • Roast and shred the chicken breast (oil, salt, 400°F / 205°C, 25 min).
  • Mix the honey-soy sauce in a jar (soy, honey, grated garlic, a little sesame oil). Slice the cucumbers.
  • Cut the bell peppers.

Prep components, not finished meals: one protein, one grain, one vegetable survives a week that changes.

Why this week

There is a particular kind of week where the plan is not the problem: you know perfectly well what to cook, you simply will not be home in time to cook it. Long recipes are not the enemy there. Any recipe at all is the enemy.

This week is built from the five fastest dinners in our rotations, and it leans on one trick: three of the five nights run on the same bag of rice — the burrito bowls, the salmon and the fried rice. That is why the grocery list asks for one 3 lb bag instead of three small ones, and why the rice is the first thing in the prep block.

How to shuffle it

  • Cook the rice once, before the week starts, for the burrito bowls and the salmon. That is the single move that makes this week work.
  • Breakfast for Dinner is the emergency night. Eggs, bread and sausages all keep, so it survives being moved to whichever evening collapses.
  • Egg Fried Rice is deliberately last, because it is the night that forgives a fridge with nothing in it. One caution: cooked rice is best eaten within three or four days, so if your prep day was the weekend, cook a fresh pot for this one on Tuesday or Wednesday rather than using rice from the prep block.
  • Leave Saturday and Sunday open on purpose. Five planned dinners and two open nights is the whole method — a seven-box plan fails by Thursday and takes the rest of the week down with it.

Being straight with you: Twenty minutes is the time on the night, with the prep block done: the rice cooked, the chicken breast roasted and shredded, the sauce mixed, the peppers cut. Without it, the quesadillas and the fried rice take considerably longer. Times assume a familiar kitchen and one person cooking — honest estimates, not a promise.
