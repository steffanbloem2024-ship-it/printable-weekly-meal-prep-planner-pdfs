On the night

Monday — Pasta e Fagioli
Soften onion, carrot and celery, add crushed tomatoes, white beans, stock and small pasta. Simmer until the pasta is done. Parmesan on top.
Tuesday — Baked Bean & Cheese Burritos
Mash refried beans with cumin, roll with rice and cheese, bake seam-down 400°F / 205°C for 15 min. Freeze the spares.
Wednesday — Pasta with Hidden-Veg Tomato Sauce + Garlic Bread
Boil the pasta. Warm the blended sauce. Butter and broil the bread.
Thursday — Egg Fried Rice
Leftover rice, frozen peas and carrots, three scrambled eggs, soy sauce, a little sesame oil.
Friday — Pizza Night
Stretch the dough onto oiled pans, sauce, cheese, bake at 475°F / 245°C for 12–14 min. Kids top their own half.

The prep block — do these once, before the week starts

  • Dice the soup vegetables all at once.
  • Cook the rice.
  • Make the sauce: soften onion, carrot and zucchini, add crushed tomatoes, simmer 20 min, blend smooth. Keeps 5 days.

Prep components, not finished meals: one protein, one grain, one vegetable survives a week that changes.

Why this week

Most “vegetarian meal plan” results assume you want to change how your family eats. This one assumes you want one week with no meat in the trolley — maybe because meat is the expensive line on the receipt, maybe because one person at the table has stopped eating it, maybe just because it is Tuesday and nobody planned anything.

So nothing here is a substitute for something else. There is no mince made of lentils pretending to be mince. The beans are in the burritos because that is what goes in a burrito, and the white beans are in the pasta e fagioli because that soup has always been beans and pasta. Two of the five nights are dinners most families already cook without noticing they contain no meat.

How to shuffle it

  • Put Pasta e Fagioli on your worst evening — it is one pot and it mostly cooks itself.
  • The bean and cheese burritos deliberately make extra. Freeze the spares flat and you have bought yourself a future open night.
  • If someone in the house is unconvinced, run Pizza Night on Friday. Nobody has ever complained that pizza is vegetarian.
  • Leave Saturday and Sunday open on purpose. Five planned dinners and two open nights is the whole method — a seven-box plan fails by Thursday and takes the rest of the week down with it.

Being straight with you: This is vegetarian in the ordinary family sense: it contains eggs, milk, butter and cheese. It is not vegan, and it is not allergen-aware — if you avoid animal rennet, check your Parmesan, and if you are cooking around an allergy, read every line before you shop.
