On the night

Monday — Taco Night
Brown the beef with taco seasoning. Put the toppings on the table and let everyone build their own.
Tuesday — Chicken Quesadillas + Tomato Soup
Fill the tortillas with shredded chicken and cheese, crisp in a dry pan. Heat the soup.
Wednesday — Pizza Night
Stretch the dough onto oiled pans, sauce, cheese, bake at 475°F / 245°C for 12–14 min. Kids top their own half.
Thursday — Breakfast for Dinner
Scrambled eggs with peppers and cheese, toast, sausages if you have them, a bowl of fruit on the table.
Friday — One-Pot Chili Mac
Brown beef and onion in a big pot, add chili powder, crushed tomatoes, beans, 2 cups stock and the dry macaroni. Simmer covered 12 min. Stir in cheese.

The prep block — do these once, before the week starts

  • Shred the cheese and lettuce; dice the tomatoes into one container.
  • Roast and shred the chicken breast (oil, salt, 400°F / 205°C, 25 min).
  • Cut the bell peppers.
  • Dice the onions.

Prep components, not finished meals: one protein, one grain, one vegetable survives a week that changes.

Why this week

The advice you get about picky eating is usually about the child. This week is about the kitchen. A dinner survives a fussy night when its parts arrive separately — when the cheese, the meat, the tortilla and the tomato are not yet a thing that has been combined without anyone’s permission.

So three of these nights are built at the table rather than plated at the stove: tacos, pizza where everyone tops their own half, and breakfast-for-dinner, which is eggs, toast, sausages and fruit arriving as four separate things. The quesadillas are here because they are the dinner most children have already agreed to. That leaves exactly one genuinely mixed dinner in the week, on purpose.

How to shuffle it

  • Taco Night is first in the week deliberately — it sets the expectation that building your own plate is just how dinner works here.
  • On Pizza Night, give each child their own half. The half is the point: it is theirs, and ownership does more than hiding vegetables ever will.
  • One-Pot Chili Mac is the mixed one. Hold back a cup of plain macaroni before the chili goes in and it becomes separable too.
  • Leave Saturday and Sunday open on purpose. Five planned dinners and two open nights is the whole method — a seven-box plan fails by Thursday and takes the rest of the week down with it.

Being straight with you: We are not child-development experts and this is not a feeding programme — it is a week of dinners arranged so a fussy night costs you nothing extra. It is omnivore, contains dairy and gluten, and is not allergen-aware.
